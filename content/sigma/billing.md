# Billing

Buy a subscription at the [VargaLABS store](https://store.vargalabs.com/). Prices are in United States dollars, shown as USD.

## Tiers and prices

| Tier | Monthly billing | Yearly billing | Purchase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | No subscription price listed | No subscription price listed | Not sold through checkout. |
| Pro | USD 19.99 per month | USD 199.99 per year | Buy in the store. |
| Team | USD 39.99 per seat per month | USD 399.99 per seat per year | Buy in the store, with at least 3 seats. |
| Enterprise | No monthly price listed | From USD 799.99 per seat per year | Contact sales through the store. |

Monthly plans bill each month. Yearly plans bill each year. A yearly price is the amount for the year, not a monthly charge.

Pro has an allowance of up to 3 machines. Team has two machines per purchased seat. See [Team setup](/sigma/team-setup/) for examples.

## Coupons

Enter a promotion code in Stripe checkout before payment. Stripe checks whether the code applies to the purchase.

| Coupon | Discount | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `LAUNCH20` | 20% off for 3 months | Enter the code at checkout. |
| `SIGMA-EDU` | 50% off for 12 months | For approved students. Enter the personal code sent by email, not the coupon name. Each issued code works once. |

Do not assume that a yearly plan receives three separate discounted invoices. Check the discount shown by Stripe before you pay.

## Free licences by application

Non-profit organisations, open source projects, and education users can apply for a free licence. Submit the [application form](https://store.vargalabs.com/apply).

Applications require review. Submission is not approval. An approved free licence is issued directly, without a Stripe subscription. Education applications can instead receive a discount code after approval.

## Manage your subscription

Use the Stripe customer portal to manage billing. Open the subscription management option in the store. Use the email address associated with your purchase.

In the portal, you can cancel at the end of the billing period or update your payment card. Use the plan-change options shown there to switch plans. The options available depend on the portal configuration.

Cancellation does not immediately revoke your licence. The store lets it expire at its recorded expiry time. A plan or seat change updates the licence allowance, but does not by itself extend paid access.

## Failed payments

A failed payment does not immediately revoke or shorten the licence. The licence expiry includes a 7-day grace period after the paid billing period ends.

The grace period is not a new 7-day period for each failed retry. Update your card in the Stripe customer portal. A successful renewal payment extends the licence to the new paid period end plus grace.
